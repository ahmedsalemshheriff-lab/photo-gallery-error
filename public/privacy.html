Guest privacy

Privacy policy.

Last updated 22 November 2026

1. No screenshots of the Gallery Wall are allowed in any manner.

This is the first rule of our privacy policy and it applies to everyone who visits Funfair Fiesta.

1. No screenshots of the Gallery Wall

The Gallery Wall shows photos of real guests. To protect them, you may not capture the Gallery Wall or any photo on it in any manner. This includes:

  • screenshots on a phone, tablet or computer, including with keyboard shortcuts, buttons or snipping tools;
  • screen recordings, screen sharing and casting the wall to another screen;
  • taking a picture of a screen showing the wall with another camera;
  • saving, downloading, copying or printing guest photos from the wall;
  • sharing or reposting any such copy anywhere, online or offline.

The Gallery Wall hides guest photos when it detects a likely screenshot, screen capture or print attempt. Not every device can be detected, so this rule still applies whether or not the photos are hidden. Event organizers may remove any copy that breaks this rule and ask the person who made it to delete it.

2. What we collect when you post

When you post to the Gallery Wall, we store your photo, the name or crew name you entered, your caption, the party size and the pose you recreated. Name and caption are optional. Only post photos that everyone pictured is happy to share.

3. Likes

To let each browser like a photo once, we set an anonymous visitor cookie. It holds a random id, not your name or any contact details.

4. Your own photo

Right after you post, you can save your own photo from your ticket. This rule covers capturing the Gallery Wall and other guests’ photos.

5. Removing a photo

To have a photo taken down, including one of you posted by someone else, ask a Funfair Fiesta organizer. Removed photos are deleted from the Gallery Wall for everyone.
